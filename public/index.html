Frequently asked questions

Do I need to already be active on social media?

No. We can start from nothing. A new account or a page that's been sitting untouched both work.

How much content do I get each month?

It depends on the package, but every plan is built around a schedule you can count on instead of one-off shoots here and there.

What if I'm not sure what I actually need?

That's normal, and it's what the first conversation is for. We'll figure out what makes sense together before anything's booked.

Do you only work with restaurants?

Not at all. We work with local businesses of all kinds in and around St. John's. Restaurants are just one type of client, not the whole focus.

Do I have to be in St. John's?

No. Shoots happen in and around St. John's, but we manage social media and design graphics remotely for businesses anywhere.

How long before I see a difference?

Consistent content takes a little time to build momentum. We'll check in regularly so you can see what's working as it happens, not just at the end.
